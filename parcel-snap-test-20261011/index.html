Tesseract auto orientation tries four separate full-photo readings with sparse-text recognition and internal deskew. It initially shows the whole reading with the highest fixed engine-confidence score; that score is not accuracy. This takes longer and needs four free reading slots. Remaining preview and text storage is divided equally across the four attempts; an oversized attempt stops the run without erasing earlier readings. Incomplete comparisons have no ranking winner. Each call requests cancellation after 90 seconds, with a six-minute comparison deadline; downloads or native cleanup may need to settle or a page reload.
