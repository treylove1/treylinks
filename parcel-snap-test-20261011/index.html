Take a clear photo or choose one from your phone. Compare every proposed field with the label and the full text below.

1. Choose a photo

One label, close enough to read the small print. Keep it sharp, well lit and as straight as possible. JPEG, PNG or WebP work best. Maximum 20 MB.

2. Read and compare

The 90° test is a separate reading. It doesn’t replace or combine the original result. Up to six readings are kept per photo. Actual reader-image previews are limited to 16 MiB each and 48 MiB in total; a new read is refused if its preview cannot be kept, with earlier evidence unchanged. Download text results before starting a new photo session. Preview images stay only in this tab and are not included in the JSON download.

Choose a photo to begin.

English Tesseract.js 6.0.1 runs on your device. Internet is needed to load the free reader assets, which can take a minute. Offline use is not guaranteed. Photos and recognized text aren’t uploaded by this page.

3. Check the result

These are unverified proposals, not accepted parcel records. This test doesn’t choose a customer or send email. 95% accuracy is the goal; it hasn’t been demonstrated.

What to check
Compare the recipient, every address line and the whole tracking number, including leading zeros. If there are two labels or two possible numbers, check the alternatives rather than trusting a guess.
